# DumosRx — Feature List

A plain list of what the app can do. Grouped by section, no walkthroughs.

## Dashboard
- Today's Sales, Total Products, Inventory Value, and Orders Today stat cards
- Action Center: low-stock alerts, pending prescriptions, overdue purchase orders, and other things needing attention, each linking straight to the relevant screen
- Recent Activity feed
- Quick Actions shortcuts to common tasks
- In-app notifications

## Point of Sale
- Barcode-scan or search-based product lookup
- Cart with quantity adjustment, per-item discounts, and manual price override
- Hold/park a transaction and resume it later
- Split payments (cash, card, transfer) with automatic change calculation
- Printable/shareable receipts
- Request-item flow for products not yet in the catalog

## Inventory
- Overview: stock health at a glance
- Catalog: full product list with category and stock-batch filtering
- Bulk product import/export via spreadsheet (multi-sheet workbook support)
- Stock Movements ledger: every stock change with its source
- Stock Audits: scheduled or ad-hoc counts with variance reporting

## Prescriptions (pharmacy stores)
- Prescription queue with status tracking
- Create/edit prescriptions with dosage, strength, and duration
- Dispense flow that deducts stock automatically
- Full audit trail per prescription

## Customers
- Customer directory with purchase history
- Per-customer activity timeline
- Loyalty Program: point accrual and redemption, configurable rules
- Store credit / customer payments tracking

## Procurement
- Vendor and supplier directory
- Purchase order creation and tracking
- Receiving flow that updates stock and creates stock batches automatically
- Requested-products queue (from POS request-item flow)

## Expenses
- Categorized expense tracking
- Add/edit/delete with an insights strip (totals by category, trends)
- Inline quick-edit from the expense table

## Reports
- Daily Close report (cash reconciliation)
- Operational Reports (sales, inventory, staff performance)
- Analytics & Insights (trends, top products, revenue breakdowns)

## Activity Log
- Store-wide audit trail of who did what, when
- Filterable by user, action type, and date
- Row-level detail view

## Settings
- Store profile, branches, and business info
- Staff accounts and role assignment
- Payment methods and receipt customization
- Register configuration
- Product units and categories
- Notifications and alert thresholds
- Security settings
- Cloud sync and account linking
- Billing and subscription management
- Local backup and restore (whole-device `.drx` export/import)

## Authentication
- PIN-based clock-in for staff
- Auto-lock after inactivity
- Multi-store and multi-staff account switching from one device

## Cross-cutting
- Works fully offline; syncs to the cloud automatically when back online
- Plan-tier feature gating (Free / Starter / Pro / Enterprise) with a store-level Loyalty Program on/off toggle
- Dark mode
- Desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux), Android app, and web dashboard, all backed by the same account
