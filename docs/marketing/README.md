# DumosRx Feature Guide

A tour of everything DumosRx can do for your store.

1. [Dashboard](dashboard.md) — your store's performance at a glance, the moment you log in.
2. [Inventory](inventory.md) — track every product, batch, and expiry date, with easy import/export.
3. [Point of Sale](pos.md) — fast, flexible checkout with held sales, discounts, and smart suggestions.
4. [Prescriptions](prescriptions.md) — pharmacy-ready prescription tracking and dispensing (Pro & Enterprise).
5. [Customers](customers.md) — a full customer directory with purchase history, loyalty, and credit tracking.
6. [Procurement](procurement.md) — manage vendors, purchase orders, and receiving (Pro & Enterprise).
7. [Expenses](expenses.md) — track operating costs with smoothed monthly reporting (Pro & Enterprise).
8. [Reports](reports.md) — Daily Close, Operational Reports, and full Analytics & Insights.
9. [Activity Log](activity-log.md) — a complete, trustworthy audit trail of everything that happens in your store.
10. [Settings](settings.md) — full control over your store's setup, staff, security, and billing.
