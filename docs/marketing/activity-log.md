# 🗂️ Activity Log

Full accountability, built in. Every meaningful action taken in your store — by any staff member — is automatically recorded and easy to review.

**Complete Audit Trail:** Every sale, expense, purchase order, stock change, prescription, and login is logged automatically the moment it happens — no extra steps for your staff, no gaps in the record.

**Search & Filter:** Quickly find what you're looking for by date range, action type, or staff member. Search across actions, tables, and staff names in seconds.

**Role-Based Visibility:** Store owners and managers see everyone's activity across the store; other staff see only their own — keeping oversight where it belongs while respecting privacy on the shop floor.

**Detailed View:** Click into any entry to see exactly what changed — who did it, when, and the full details of the record involved, in plain, human-readable language.

Whether you're reconciling the day's activity, investigating a discrepancy, or just keeping tabs on how your store runs, the Activity Log gives you a complete, trustworthy record — always available, never edited after the fact.
