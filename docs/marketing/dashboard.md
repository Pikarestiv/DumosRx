# 📊 Dashboard

The moment you log in, DumosRx gives you a clear picture of how your store is doing — no digging required.

**Today's Sales:** See your net revenue for the day at a glance, with a quick comparison to yesterday so you always know if business is trending up or down.

**Total Products & Inventory Value:** Know exactly how many products you carry, across how many categories, and what your entire stock is worth in Naira — updated automatically as you buy and sell.

**Orders Today:** A running count of completed sales, so you can feel the pace of the day without leaving the dashboard.

**Action Center:** Store owners and managers get a smart alert strip that surfaces what needs attention right now — low stock warnings, batches missing expiry dates, an incomplete store profile, trial/subscription reminders, and more. Tap any alert to jump straight to where you can fix it.

**Recent Activity:** A live feed of what's happening in your store — sales, expenses, purchase orders, prescriptions, and stock changes — so you can stay on top of things even when you're not on the shop floor.

**Quick Actions:** One-tap shortcuts to the things you do most: start a new sale, add stock, close the register, scan a barcode, or jump to Customers and Reports.

**Notifications:** A bell icon keeps you posted on account-level activity as it happens.

Everything on the Dashboard updates in real time, so whether you're checking in from the shop floor or from your phone at home, you always know where your store stands.
