# 💸 Expenses

Know exactly where your money is going — DumosRx makes tracking operating costs quick and clear, available on our Pro and Enterprise plans.

**Simple Expense Logging:** Record expenses in seconds — category, description, amount, date, and payment method. Categories cover the essentials: Rent, Utilities, Salaries, Maintenance, Marketing, and more.

**Spread-Out Costs:** For expenses that cover more than one month — like a prepaid lease or an annual subscription — tell DumosRx how many months it covers, and it automatically smooths the cost across that period in your reports, so your monthly numbers always reflect real spending, not one-time spikes.

**At-a-Glance Insights:** See your all-time total expenses, this month's spending, and your top spending category, updated the moment you add or edit an expense.

**Search & Filter:** Quickly find any expense by category or description.

**Edit & Delete:** Update or remove an expense anytime, with a clear confirmation step so nothing is deleted by accident.

With DumosRx, your operating costs are always current, accurately categorized, and easy to review — so you know exactly what it costs to keep your store running.
