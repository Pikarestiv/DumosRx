# 📦 Inventory

DumosRx keeps your entire stock organized, accurate, and easy to manage — from a single glance at your dashboard down to every batch and expiry date on the shelf.

**Overview at a glance:** See total stock value, total products, low-stock counts, and what's expiring soon — all in one place. A "Needs attention" panel flags your worst-off items first, whether that's a batch about to expire or a product that's run out, so you know exactly what to reorder and when.

**Fast movers:** Instantly see your top sellers from the last 7 days, so you always know what's flying off the shelves.

**Product Catalog:** Manage your full list of products — names, categories, cost and selling prices, and stock counts — with instant search and sortable columns. Filter by category, stock status (Low Stock, Out of Stock, Expiring Soon, and more), or active/inactive status to find exactly what you need in seconds.

**Batch & Expiry Tracking:** Never lose money to expired goods. DumosRx tracks the exact batch numbers and expiry dates for every item in your store, so you always know what needs to move first.

**Bulk Import & Export:** Bring your entire product catalog in from a spreadsheet — even multi-sheet workbooks — with smart column matching that recognizes common formats like QuickBooks POS and Moniebook exports. Export your catalog anytime as CSV or Excel, with the option to choose exactly which columns you need.

**Stock Movements:** A complete, tamper-proof log of every stock change in your store — sales, restocks, returns, damage, and adjustments — searchable and filterable by date, type, product, or staff member.

**Cycle Counts (Physical Inventory Audits):** Reconcile your shelves against the system anytime with a guided count. DumosRx syncs to the latest stock levels first, lets you enter counted quantities product by product, shows you exactly what changed, and logs every adjustment for a clean audit trail.

With DumosRx, you always know what you have, what it's worth, and what needs your attention next.
