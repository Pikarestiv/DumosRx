# 🛒 Point of Sale (POS)

Built for speed at the counter, DumosRx's POS lets your cashiers ring up sales quickly and accurately, every time.

**Fast Product Search & Selection:** Search by name or SKU, filter by category, or scan a barcode to add items instantly. Out-of-stock items are clearly marked so nothing gets sold that you don't actually have.

**Smart Suggestions:** Once a customer's cart has an item in it, DumosRx recommends related or frequently-bought-together products — a simple way to increase your average sale.

**Recently Sold:** Quickly re-add items your store has just sold, with up-to-date stock counts right on the card.

**Flexible Cart:** Adjust quantities with a tap, apply fixed or percentage discounts, attach a customer to the sale, and swipe or click to remove items — all reflected instantly in the running total.

**Park Transactions (Hold & Recall):** Put a customer's cart on hold if they need to step away, and pick up serving the next person in line without losing a thing. When you recall a held sale, every item, quantity, and discount comes back exactly as it was.

**Split & Flexible Payments:** Accept Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, Credit, or a Mix of methods on a single sale. Cash payments calculate exact change automatically, and card/transfer sales can be tied to a specific destination account for cleaner reconciliation.

**Customer Credit Sales:** Let trusted customers pay later — credit sales are tracked against their account balance automatically.

**Custom Receipts:** Print thermal receipts branded with your store's logo and details, showing a clean breakdown of items, discounts, tax, and payment.

**Missing Product Log:** When a customer asks for something you don't stock, your cashier can log the request in seconds — visible to whoever manages your purchasing, so you never miss a chance to stock what your customers actually want.

Every sale — whether paid in cash, by card, on credit, or held and resumed later — is tracked accurately down to the last unit of stock, so your inventory numbers are always trustworthy.
