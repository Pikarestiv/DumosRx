# 🏥 Prescriptions

Built specifically for pharmacies, DumosRx's Prescriptions module helps you manage patient prescriptions from intake to dispensing — available on our Pro and Enterprise plans for stores operating as pharmacies.

**Prescription Registry:** Digitally record every prescription — patient details, prescribing doctor, license number, medications, dosage, quantity, and refill schedule — replacing paper records with a searchable digital queue.

**Status Tracking:** Follow every prescription through a clear pipeline — Needs Verification → In Progress → Ready for Pickup → Dispensed — with filter chips and search so your pharmacy team always knows what's next.

**Seamless Dispensing:** When it's time to dispense, DumosRx hands the prescription straight into checkout with the correct medications and quantities pre-loaded — so your pharmacist can complete the sale, deduct stock, and close out the prescription in one smooth motion.

**Refills:** Track authorized refills and refill intervals right alongside each prescription, so repeat patients are easy to serve.

**Returns:** If a dispensed prescription needs to be reversed, DumosRx routes you straight to the original sale so stock and payment are corrected properly.

Prescriptions in DumosRx isn't a bolt-on feature — it's built into the same reliable sales and inventory engine that powers the rest of your store, so every dispensed medication is tracked accurately, every time.
