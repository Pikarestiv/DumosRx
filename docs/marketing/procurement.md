# 📋 Procurement

Keep your shelves stocked and your supplier relationships organized — DumosRx's Procurement tools are available on our Pro and Enterprise plans.

**Vendors & Suppliers:** Keep a full directory of your suppliers — contact details, payment terms, and a running history of every order you've placed with them, including total value and any outstanding balance you owe.

**Purchase Orders:** Create purchase orders in a few clicks, either as a standard order to send to a supplier or as an Immediate Purchase for walk-in/self buys where goods arrive on the spot. Add line items by searching your existing catalog or creating a brand-new product on the fly. Track every order's status — Draft, Sent, or Received — from one dashboard.

**Receiving Made Simple:** When goods arrive, receiving them updates your stock automatically — batch numbers, expiry dates, and costs all flow straight into Inventory. Partial deliveries are handled correctly too: only receive what actually showed up, and your stock reflects exactly that.

**Supplier Debt Ledger:** Track goods received on credit and manage your cash flow with confidence, logging partial or full payments against outstanding orders.

**Requested Products:** When a cashier logs a product a customer asked for but you didn't have, it lands straight on your Procurement desk — so you always know what your customers want next.

Procurement in DumosRx turns the paperwork of restocking into a few clean clicks, with your inventory numbers updating automatically the moment goods come through the door.
