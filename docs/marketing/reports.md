# 📈 Reports

DumosRx turns your store's raw activity into clear, actionable numbers — so you always know how your business is really doing.

**Daily Close:** A single-day reconciliation view built for closing out a shift. See total sales, expected cash, transfers, refunds, and estimated profit at a glance, plus a full payment-method breakdown and your top-selling products for the day. Every figure links straight through to the underlying transactions, so nothing is a mystery number.

**Operational Reports:** Six ready-to-go report types — Detailed Sales, Inventory Valuation, Profit & Loss Summary, Customer Loyalty, Expense Categories, and Top Sellers — each filterable by date range, staff member, and payment method, and exportable as PDF or CSV with one click. Your recent downloads are always a click away.

**Analytics & Insights:** A full business-intelligence dashboard for store owners — net sales, net profit, transaction counts, stock value, and active customers, front and center. Dig deeper with dedicated views for Sales Analytics, Profit & Loss, Stock Batch Insights, Customer Behaviour, and Staff Performance, complete with trend charts and category breakdowns.

**Print & Export, anywhere:** Every report can be downloaded or printed on the spot, so whether it's for your accountant, your files, or a quick check on the go, your numbers are always ready.

DumosRx gives every store owner the same clear window into sales, profit, and performance that used to take a spreadsheet and a weekend to put together — updated in real time, every day.
