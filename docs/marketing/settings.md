# ⚙️ Settings

DumosRx gives you complete control over how your store looks, operates, and stays secure — all from one organized Settings area.

**Appearance:** Choose Light, Dark, or System theme, pick from named color palettes, and customize your sidebar behavior — your device, your preference.

**Business Info:** Set your business vertical (Pharmacy, Grocery, Supermarket, or General — unlocking the right terminology and modules for your business), manage your business details and logo, and configure your public storefront if you sell online.

**Branches:** Managing more than one location? Add and manage every store branch on your account from a single screen.

**Staff & Role-Based Access:** Create accounts for your team and assign each one a role — Admin, Manager, Specialist, Cashier, or Auditor — that determines exactly what they can see and do in the app. Update roles anytime as your team grows.

**Payment Methods:** Turn on the payment types your store accepts — Cash, Card, Transfer, Credit, and Mixed — and manage your destination bank/terminal accounts for clean reconciliation.

**Receipt Settings:** Customize your printed receipts — paper size, footer messages, and what shows on every printout — with a live preview as you edit.

**Register Configs:** Fine-tune checkout behavior, like requiring a sale note or showing stock levels to cashiers as they sell.

**Product Units & Categories:** Add your own custom units and categories on top of DumosRx's built-in lists, tailored to exactly how your store organizes its catalog.

**Notifications:** Configure low-stock and expiry warnings so you're alerted before a shortage or expiration becomes a problem.

**Data & Sync:** See your cloud sync status at a glance, control how often your data syncs automatically (on Pro and Enterprise plans), and download a local backup anytime for extra peace of mind.

**Security:** Change your login PIN and set an auto-lock timer to keep your store's terminal secure when it's unattended.

**Billing:** Manage your subscription plan, review billing history, and access your referral program — all in one place.

Every setting you change takes effect immediately. Store-wide settings — staff roles, receipts, pricing, sync — travel with your store, so your team stays on the same configuration wherever they log in. A few, like your theme and auto-lock timer, are yours alone, by design, so each device can be set up the way you like it.
