D U M O S R X
The problem

Pharmacies pay for a POS and a website that never talk to each other, and neither copes with a bad network day.

The till stops

Most POS software needs a constant connection. When internet or power drops, sales stop, or the owner falls back to paper that never gets reconciled.

Data gets lost

Offline-only systems like out-of-support QuickBooks 2013 lose records when a machine fails, with no cloud backup and no view across branches.

Paying twice

On top of the POS, owners buy a website and renew it every year, and it knows nothing about what is actually in stock.

What it costs them

Missed sales, stock that drifts from the shelf, drugs found expired too late, and no way to watch staff at a second branch.

Why current options fall short

Imported tools assume stable infrastructure. Local ones are offline-only, outdated, or lack pharmacy-specific stock handling.

02
D U M O S R X
The solution

A POS that works without internet, with the online shop built in.

Offline-first POS

Every till runs on a local database. Sales, stock and receipts work with no connection and sync to the cloud automatically when it returns. Desktop, mobile and web from one codebase.

Pharmacy-grade stock

Batch-level inventory with low-stock alerts and expiry tracking, plus procurement, prescriptions, customers, loyalty and expenses.

Online storefront included

Each store gets its own online shop on the same catalog, in one subscription. No separate website to build, host and renew.

Owner visibility

A cloud dashboard shows sales, stock and staff activity across every branch, so owners can monitor remotely and catch fraud.
